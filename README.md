# Staniforth Road — A house, reimagined

A personal five-minute film tracing the house renovation from September 2021 to March 2026, in seven chapters. This edition includes 50 full-frame media shots, 12 stabilized original video excerpts at 30fps, cinematic character-by-character text reveals and seven animated geometric chapter portraits.

The site uses Astro. It includes adaptive 1080p/720p HLS playback, a downloadable 1080p MP4, chapter navigation, sharing and the seven full-resolution chapter pictures. The earlier two-minute MP4 remains available at its original media URL.

Current soundtrack: user-selected “I Love to Love” remix from the personal music collection, source section 10:58–17:54 of the longer recording. The film uses its opening five minutes with a closing fade. The recording has gentle hiss and rumble cleanup.

Earlier two-minute edition music: [“Ascension” by Scott Buckley](https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/ascension/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), edited excerpt with fades.

Visual study: [Fox Geometric Camo by Matthieu Martigny / Mattmart](https://dribbble.com/shots/27837272-Fox-Geometric-Camo) and [Smertimba Graphics’ cinematic text tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eZI6E2jApw). The house illustrations are original architectural compositions; the reference artwork and tutorial footage are not included in this website or film.

Streaming uses hls.js 1.7.3. Its licence is included in `public/vendor/hls-LICENSE.txt`.

Photography remains the property of its owner. No reuse licence is granted for the personal photographs or film.

The latest edition adds March 2022 and April 2025 source footage and expands the March 2026 interior chapter. Camera footage is stabilized with feature tracking and smoothed camera paths; the film uses 30fps to preserve the original video cadence.

Eight of the fastest handheld pans are presented as sharp source-frame holds with restrained camera movement.
